Privacy Policy

Uploadable · Last updated 16 August 2026

Uploadable collects nothing about you, and your photo never leaves your phone. That is the whole policy. The rest of this page is the specifics, because for an app you hand a passport photo to, the details are the part that matters.

Your photo never leaves your phone

Every photo you open is read, cropped, resized and written on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is uploaded. No copy is sent anywhere, at any point, for any reason. There is no server to send it to.

This is worth being concrete about, because you are handing this app a photograph of your face that you are about to attach to a visa or passport application. It is processed entirely on the device in front of you, and it works with Airplane Mode switched on — which is the simplest way to prove it to yourself.

There are no servers

Uploadable makes no network requests. There is no backend and no API. The app contains no networking code at all.

There are no accounts

You do not sign up and you do not sign in. Uploadable never asks for your email address, your name, or any other detail about you.

There is no analytics

No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no tracking of any kind. Uploadable also has no third-party SDKs — it is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks, with zero outside dependencies.

Location data is removed, not just ignored

Photos taken on a phone usually carry the place they were taken, along with the camera, the timestamp and other EXIF data. Uploadable strips all of it from the file it produces, so what you upload to a government form carries your photograph and nothing else. The app re-reads the finished file and checks the location data is genuinely gone before handing it to you.

What is stored

One number: how many of your free exports you have used. It lives in the app's own storage on your device and is mirrored in the iOS Keychain, so that reinstalling does not hand out a fresh allowance. It is never transmitted, and it says nothing about which photos you used.

Finished photos are saved to Files → On My iPhone → Uploadable when you ask for them, and stay there until you delete them.

Purchases go through Apple

Uploadable Pro is sold with Apple's StoreKit. Apple takes the payment and tells the app one thing: whether the purchase exists. We never see your card details, your Apple Account, or your name. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple verifies this

The app ships a privacy manifest — PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy — declaring no data collection and no tracking. Apple checks that manifest against the app when it is submitted, so this is not only our word for it.

Children

Uploadable collects no data from anyone, which includes children. There is nothing about the app that treats a child's use differently, because there is nothing to treat differently.

If this ever changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so plainly and the change will be described in the App Store release notes before you update. We would rather tell you than bury it here.
